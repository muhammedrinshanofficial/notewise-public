    Newton's Laws of Motion

    Summary

    This lecture introduces the three laws of motion and shows how each one explains everyday situations, from a book resting on a table to a rocket leaving the ground.

    Key Topics

    First law: inertia
    • An object keeps its state of motion unless a net force acts on it.
    • Rest and constant velocity are treated as the same condition.
    Second law: force and acceleration
    • Force equals mass times acceleration, so the same force moves a lighter object faster.
    • The direction of the acceleration matches the direction of the net force.
    Third law: action and reaction
    • Forces always come in pairs acting on two different objects.
    • A rocket accelerates because it pushes exhaust gases backwards.
